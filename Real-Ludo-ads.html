REAL LUDO

How to play

  • You need a 6 to bring a piece out of the yard.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing a piece or reaching home gives you another roll.
  • Three sixes in a row cancel your turn.
  • Coloured start squares and star squares are safe. Pieces cannot be captured there.
  • Two pieces of the same colour on one square form a block and are safe too.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre.
  • Bring all four of your pieces home first to win.
